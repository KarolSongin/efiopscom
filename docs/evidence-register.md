The editable case contract distinguishes project type (Internal, Owned-business, Client, Demonstration) from project status (Completed, In progress, Pilot, Concept). `permissionConfirmed` and `evidenceReviewed` are independently required. Metrics require a method, denominator and observation period. Internal evidence notes are excluded from public rendering even after an entry is approved.

The article draft is an editorial assignment, not finished content. Publication requires reviewed original guidance, sources and real dates. The build does not change any external task statuses or claim employer/vendor endorsements.
